THE HAMMER by APT Industrial - installing on Windows / FL Studio
=================================================================

1. Copy these three folders into  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
      The Hammer.vst3        (drummer instrument - Channel Rack)
      The Hammer FX.vst3     (drummer as a mixer insert)
      The Hammer Ears.vst3   (listener for the drummer instrument)

2. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins (tick "Verify plugins").
   Star them so they show up in the plugin browser.

3. Kits (optional but recommended): download TheHammer-Kits.zip from the
   GitHub Releases page and unzip the folders into
      Documents\APT Industrial\The Hammer\Kits
   The Hammer loads them automatically. The built-in modeled kits work without them.

QUICK SETUP (Drummer + Ears)
  * Channel Rack: add "The Hammer" and send it to a free mixer track.
  * Mixer: put "The Hammer Ears" on a track/bus carrying your song WITHOUT drums.
  * Same LINK CHANNEL on both. Press play. EARS LED = green.

QUICK SETUP (one plugin)
  * Mixer: put "The Hammer FX" on the track/bus carrying your song. Press play.

"The Hammer.exe" is a standalone version (press PLAY in its header).
Open the ROAD CREW tab inside the plugin for the full guide and MIDI keys.
