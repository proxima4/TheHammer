title: The main blast beats - traditional, bomb, hammer, skank
style: death metal
tempo: 220

Standard extreme-metal vocabulary (the blast-beat family played by drummers
like Frost of 1349 / Satyricon). Ride or crash plays with the kick.

Skank beat (the sped-up polka / thrash beat blasts grew from)
Rd  |x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-|
SN  |--O---O---O---O-|
B   |o---o---o---o---|  x4

Traditional blast (kick and snare alternate, cymbal with the kick)
CC  |x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-|
SN  |-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o|
B   |o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-|  x4

Bomb blast (16th kicks under an 8th-note snare)
CC  |x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-|
SN  |-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o|
B   |oooooooooooooooo|  x4

Hammer blast (kick and snare together)
CC  |x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-|
SN  |o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-|
B   |o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-|  x4
