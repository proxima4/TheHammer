YOUR GROOVES -> THE HAMMER'S NEURAL BRAIN
=========================================
Put MIDI drum files (General MIDI drum notes, 4/4) in the folder of the style
they belong to, e.g.  metalcore/breakdown_01.mid , cajon/rumba_take3.mid

  jazz  rock  metalcore  death metal  indie  fusion jazz
  funk  groove  blues  orchestral  minimal  cajon

* Files whose name contains "fill" teach the fill mode.
* Your files are weighted x4 against the Groove MIDI Dataset.
* Commit them, then run Actions > "Train the neural brain" again.

Best material: your own e-kit recordings or programmed parts (humanised
timing and velocity make the best teachers). Transcriptions of your own
recordings work too. Don't use performances ripped from other people's videos.

Cajon folder: kick = bass tone, snare = slap, side stick (37) = finger tip,
shaker (70) / tambourine (54) as usual.
Orchestral: kick = concert bass drum, toms = timpani, ride = suspended cymbal,
crash = clash cymbals, china = gong.
